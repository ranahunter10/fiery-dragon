## What the capture wrote and what it cost p15d

- opens expected 4,400,000; OPEN records with their path 4,400,000; PATH_MISSING records of the workload 0; CLOSE records 4,400,000; lost to the ring 0
- records per open+close: 2.000; bytes per open+close in the WAL: 171.6 (OPEN 115.6, CLOSE 56.0)
- opens carrying the object's facts: 9.1%
- opens by other processes during the traced arms: 561
- p15d idle: 1.17 ms of CPU per second (mean; median 0.96)
- p15d per open+close of the workload: 237.2 ns of CPU in the window (mean), 231.9 ns after the idle rate is taken off (median 221.2 ns)
- p15d's thread switches: 0.4 per 1,000 open+close in the window (median), 9.8 per second idle (median)
- agent overhead of the opens (p15d's CPU above its idle rate / the two open events' CPU time without capture): mean 24.9%, median 23.4%, range 19.7% to 39.0%
- agent overhead of the whole workload, as eAudit defines it (all of p15d's CPU in the window / the workload's CPU time without capture, null syscalls included): mean 17.7%, median 16.5%
